5 Cute Cardigan Sweater Outfit Ideas for Fall

Easy, comfortable and stylish looks for your everyday wardrobe.

Cardigans are a timeless wardrobe favorite. From classic knit styles to patterned and striped designs, they're easy to layer with jeans, trousers and sneakers. Here are five simple outfit ideas to inspire your next look.

1. Classic Cardigan and Blue Jeans

Pair a neutral cardigan with blue jeans and clean white sneakers for a relaxed outfit that works for shopping, coffee dates and casual days.

Style formula: Knit cardigan + blue jeans + white sneakers.

2. Striped Cardigan and Straight-Leg Jeans

A striped cardigan adds interest without making an outfit complicated. Keep the rest of your look simple with straight-leg jeans and a small shoulder bag.

Style formula: Striped cardigan + simple top + straight-leg jeans.

3. Argyle Cardigan and Tailored Trousers

Try a diamond-patterned argyle cardigan for a classic, preppy look. Add tailored trousers and loafers for a polished everyday outfit.

Style formula: Argyle cardigan + tailored trousers + loafers.

4. Chunky Cardigan and a Basic T-Shirt

Layer an oversized cardigan over a basic T-shirt. Balance the cozy shape with slim or straight-leg jeans and simple accessories.

Style formula: Chunky cardigan + basic tee + everyday jeans.

5. Cardigan and a Simple Skirt

For a softer look, wear a fitted cardigan with a midi skirt and ankle boots or ballet flats. Choose complementary colors for an easy, coordinated outfit.

Style formula: Fitted cardigan + midi skirt + ankle boots.

Quick styling tip: Start with neutral colors such as cream, brown, gray and navy. Add a patterned cardigan when you want your outfit to stand out.
